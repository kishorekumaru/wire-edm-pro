## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Three dedicated service pages
The site SHALL provide three service pages: Wire EDM / Wire Cutting, Milling Solutions, and Turning Solutions — each reachable from the main navigation and from the home page services overview.

#### Scenario: Direct access
- **WHEN** a visitor opens any service page URL directly
- **THEN** the full page renders with shared header/footer and its own title and meta description

### Requirement: Consistent service page structure
Each service page SHALL follow a consistent structure: service hero (name + value proposition), capability details (processes, achievable tolerances/precision, materials), industry applications (manufacturing, mining, medical as relevant), and an enquiry CTA section.

#### Scenario: Structure parity
- **WHEN** the three service pages are compared
- **THEN** each contains the hero, capabilities, applications, and CTA sections in the same order with page-specific content

### Requirement: Wire EDM page depth
The Wire EDM / Wire Cutting page SHALL be the most detailed service page, covering wire EDM and EDM cutting capabilities, precision characteristics (fine tolerances, intricate profiles, hardened materials), and when to choose wire EDM over conventional machining.

#### Scenario: Wire EDM differentiation content
- **WHEN** a visitor reads the Wire EDM page
- **THEN** they find content explaining what wire EDM is, what precision it achieves, and which use cases suit it

### Requirement: Enquiry CTAs on every service page
Every service page SHALL end with a call-to-action section linking to the Contact page.

#### Scenario: CTA navigation
- **WHEN** a visitor clicks the CTA at the end of a service page
- **THEN** they arrive at the Contact Us page
