## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Turnkey lifecycle flow
The site SHALL present the turnkey delivery lifecycle as an ordered step sequence: (1) customer proposal or idea — a verbal flow is enough, (2) design in Mastercam, (3) design approval, (4) material procurement, (5) manufacturing and processing, (6) quality control, (7) delivery — framed on quick turnaround. It SHALL be a shared component rendered on the Home page and the Reverse Engineering page.

#### Scenario: Home page lifecycle
- **WHEN** a visitor scrolls the home page
- **THEN** they find the seven-step turnkey flow in order with a quick-turnaround statement

#### Scenario: Reused on Reverse Engineering
- **WHEN** a visitor reads the Reverse Engineering page
- **THEN** the same lifecycle section appears after the measure-and-design explanation
